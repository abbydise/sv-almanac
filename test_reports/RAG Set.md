## Ambiguous
1. What's a good gift for Sebastian?
	1. It depends on your goal, but Sebastian’s loved gifts in the context are Frog Egg, Obsidian, Pumpkin Soup, Sashimi, Stardrop Tea, Void Egg, and Jack Be Nimble, Jack Be Thick. ✅
2. What crop should I plant in spring?
	1. It depends on your goals and preferences. In Spring, valid options include Parsnip Seeds, Bean Starter, Cauliflower Seeds, Jazz Seeds, Garlic Seeds, and Rice Shoot from Pierre’s General Store, and Spring Seeds can yield Daffodil, Dandelion, or Leek. If you want a bundle crop, the Spring Crops Bundle requires Parsnip, Green Bean, Cauliflower, and Potato. If you are trying to get three strawberry harvests in Year 1, the context says to plant strawberries on Spring 13 with Speed-Gro, after completing the spring crops bundle. ✅
3. Can I get to Skull Cavern?
	1. Yes. The Skull Cavern is opened by using the Skull Key to open a door in a cave in the Desert. The Skull Key is obtained on floor 120 of the Mines. ✅
4. Should I upgrade my house first or my tools?
	1. It depends on your goals and preferences. If you want more space and cooking access, the first farmhouse upgrade increases house size and adds a Kitchen, costs 10,000g and 450 Wood, and takes three days to complete. If you want better farming and mining efficiency, tool upgrades are available at the Blacksmith, take two days each, and must be done in ascending order of materials, with the first upgrades costing 2,000g and 5 Copper Bars for each main tool, or 1,000g for the Copper Trash Can. ✅
5. How do I get more energy?
	1. You can get more energy by eating food. Most crops and many foraged items can be eaten to regain energy, and some foods can also subtract energy, so you should check the energy amount by hovering over the food. ✅
6. Is the Community Center or Joja Mart better?
	1. It depends on your goals and preferences. If you want to restore the Community Center, completing all bundles leads to the Community Center being finished, JojaMart going out of business, Pierre's General Store being open every day of the week, and the Blacksmith shop being closed on Fridays because Clint spends all day at the Community Center. If you purchase a Joja membership instead, you can buy Joja Community Development Form projects, JojaMart prices become the same as Pierre's for most items, and the Joja Warehouse is built after the membership purchase. ✅

8. Should I choose a dog or a cat?
	1. It depends on your preference. The player is allowed to adopt either a cat or a dog, depending on which was chosen on the character creation screen, and there are several different styles of cat and dog. ✅
9. What's the best farm layout?
	1. It depends on your goals and preferences. The valid options in the context are, Standard Farm, which is designed to maximize open space for crops and animals, Forest Farm, which limits farming space but has renewable stumps and seasonal forage items, Riverland Farm, where water significantly decreases farming area but fishing is viable, Meadowlands Farm, which encourages farming, specifically raising animals, Hill-top Farm, which includes a small quarry and favors multiplayer, and Beach Farm, which offers good foraging and fishing with a lot of open space. ✅
10. How do I get iridium?
	1. You can get iridium by mining Iridium Nodes and Mystic Stones, by breaking crates and barrels in the dangerous Mines and Volcano Dungeon, by catching regular and golden Fishing Treasure Chests, by panning in any area, by using the Statue of Perfection, by mining a fallen meteorite on the Farm, by buying it from the Traveling Cart, by getting it from a Super Cucumber Fish Pond at population 9, by getting it from Iridium Crabs, Iridium Golems, Purple Slimes, Shadow Shamans, Shadow Brutes, Iridium Bats, or Wilderness Golems, and by receiving it from the Statue of Endless Fortune on days that are not a villager’s birthday. ✅
11. What happens at the flower dance?
	1. At the Flower Dance, the player can enter Cindersap Forest between 9 AM and 2 PM on Spring 24 to attend the festival. If the player finds a partner, they can participate in the dance, and dancing increases friendship with that partner by 1 heart, or 250 points. The dance begins after talking to Lewis, and the festival ends after the dance is performed. ✅
12. Should I use sprinklers or water crops by hand?
	1. It depends on your goals and preferences. Sprinklers automatically water crops every morning at 6am, which frees you from daily watering, but they require crafted and placed sprinklers and may sacrifice some crop tiles. Watering by hand with a Watering Can uses no crop space, but crops need to be watered every day, and a crop plant does not grow on a day it is not watered. If you want to avoid sprinklers entirely, Deluxe Retaining Soil is an alternative because it has a 100% chance of staying watered overnight and can eliminate the need for sprinklers. ✅
